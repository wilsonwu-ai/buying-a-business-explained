# Buying a business, explained

How a small-business acquisition gets paid for: the capital stack, how a leveraged buyout pays itself down, the 5% own-cash rule (as one SBA loan broker reads the new SBA rules), what equity investors expect, and why a lower price often beats raising more money.

**Read it here:** https://wilsonwu-ai.github.io/buying-a-business-explained/

- `docs/index.html`: the explainer, with animated diagrams and a narrated video (about 2 minutes)
- `docs/article.html`: the full article, "From 0 to 0.5"
- `model/worked_example.py`: every worked-example number on the site comes from this script (`python3 model/worked_example.py` regenerates `worked_example.md` and `.json`); market and rule numbers link to their public sources
- `build.py`: rebuilds `docs/` from `content/` (the copy) and `templates/` (the page shells)

The worked example is an illustrative company, not a real deal. SBA rule points follow one SBA loan broker's reading of SOP 50 10 8.1; confirm them with your lender.
